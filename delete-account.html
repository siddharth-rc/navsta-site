How to delete your Navsta account and the data linked to it

In the app

  1. Open Navsta and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email sidrcx@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Navsta account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email address, profile picture, bike and phone number
  • Your emergency contacts
  • Your live location and your membership of every trip
  • Trips you own that have no other members, with their routes, stops and checklists
  • Expenses, payments, fuel logs, checklist items and alerts you added to any trip

What is kept

  • Trips you own that have other members are handed to another member so the group keeps its plan. The route and stops stay with the trip; your membership, location, expenses and other personal data are removed from it.
  • Copies in encrypted server backups can remain for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
