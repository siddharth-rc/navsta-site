NAVSTA

Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains what the Navsta mobile app ("Navsta", "we") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Navsta is a companion app for group motorcycle rides.

Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Name, email address and profile pictureWhen you sign in with Google or with an email linkTo create your account and show your name to riders in trips you join
Bike model and phone number (optional)If you add them to your profileShown to members of trips you join, so they can recognise and call you
Trips, stops, routes, checklists and invite codesWhen you plan or join a rideTo run the trip for its members
Precise location, heading and speedOnly while you have turned on location sharing during an active rideTo show your position to members of that ride and compute distance and ETA
SOS and breakdown alerts, with your location at that momentWhen you raise oneTo alert the members of your ride and record who responded
Expenses, payments and fuel logsWhen you add themTo split costs between trip members and estimate fuel range
Emergency contactsIf you add themSo you can call or text them from the SOS screen. They are visible only to you.

Location

Navsta uses your device's precise location only while you share it during an active ride. While sharing on Android a persistent notification is shown, and you can stop at any time. Your position is sent to members of that ride and only your latest position is stored — not a history of where you have been. Stored positions are deleted when the ride is completed. We do not use your location for advertising and we do not sell it.

Information from Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google Account (the openid, email and profile scopes). We use them only to create and identify your Navsta account. Navsta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How information is shared

  • Other riders: members of a trip you join see your name, bike, phone number (if provided), live location while you share it, alerts you raise and the trip's expenses. People who are not members of the trip cannot see any of it.
  • Service providers that run Navsta for us, under their own privacy terms:
    • Supabase — authentication, database, realtime messaging and file storage.
    • Google — Sign in with Google, and Google Maps Platform for maps, place search and routing.
    • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts for ride locations (only coordinates are sent).
    • OpenStreetMap-based services (OSRM, Photon) — used as a fallback for routing and place search (only coordinates or search text are sent).
  • Apps you choose to open: when you tap navigate, call, text or share, Navsta hands the relevant details to the app you choose (for example your maps or phone app).
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect the safety of people.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and trip data is kept while your account exists. Live positions are deleted when a ride ends. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account). This deletes your profile, emergency contacts, location data and memberships, and your own trip records; shared trips you own are handed to another member so the group keeps its plan. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing sidrcx@gmail.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted at the database level so that only members of a trip can read that trip's data, and only you can read your emergency contacts.

Children

Navsta is intended for adults who ride motorcycles and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Turn location sharing on or off at any time during a ride, or revoke the location permission in your device settings.
  • Edit or remove your bike and phone number in Profile.
  • Delete your account in the app.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: sidrcx@gmail.com
